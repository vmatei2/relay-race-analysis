Sources, timing precision & interpretation

The {{ report.count * 2 }} completed splits come from Tracklife’s results and Will-Being’s results. Empty “Lap 30” rows are excluded. Runner attribution is supplied by the timing provider.

For exact reconciliation, lap durations are differences between published cumulative times. The separately displayed “Leg Time” can differ by one second ({{ report.rounding_rows }} rows in this dataset). Both fields are preserved in the full lap CSV. The primary charts and narrative consistently use cumulative-derived durations.

Pace assumes {{ race.lap_distance_km }} km per lap; the team confirmed the opening lap followed the same route and distance. No within-lap splits, GPS, heart rate or separate handover measurements are available. Slower laps may have several causes; the data shows timing changes, not their cause.
