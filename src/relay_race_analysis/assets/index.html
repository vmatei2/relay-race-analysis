Where the times come from

We took the {{ report.count * 2 }} completed lap records from Tracklife’s results and Will-Being’s results. The results list each runner beside their lap. We left out the empty “Lap 30” rows.

We calculated each lap’s duration by subtracting the previous lap’s finish time from its finish time. That makes the gains and losses add up to the official {{ report.margin }}-second margin. The results site’s separate “Leg Time” differs by one second on {{ report.rounding_rows }} laps; the full lap CSV includes both versions.

Pace uses {{ race.lap_distance_km }} km for every lap. The team confirmed that the opening lap covered the same route and distance. We only have finish-line timings, so we can’t separate running time from handover delays or explain what caused a slower lap.
